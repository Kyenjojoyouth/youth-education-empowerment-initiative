Who we are

Education can open doors. Empowerment can help young people walk through them.

We believe young people deserve encouragement, useful skills and opportunities to participate positively in their communities.

Youth Education and Empowerment Initiative is being developed around a simple goal: to support young people through education-focused efforts, mentorship, skills development and community collaboration. We want to listen to local needs and work responsibly with people and organisations who share this vision.

Our current base is Rugaya Trading Centre, Kyarusozi Sub-county, Kyenjojo District, Uganda.
