Local commitment. Shared opportunity.

Helping young people learn, grow and shape their future.

Youth Education and Empowerment Initiative is an emerging community initiative based at Rugaya Trading Centre, Kyarusozi Sub-county, Kyenjojo District, Uganda. We are working toward better educational opportunities, practical skills and positive pathways for young people.

Early-stage initiative

Building a strong foundation

We are developing the initiative and preparing for formal registration. We do not present ourselves as a registered organisation at this stage.

Our starting point: community needs, clear plans, responsible partnerships and honest reporting.

Who we are

Opportunity should reach young people in every community.

We believe education and practical empowerment can help young people build confidence, strengthen their livelihoods and contribute positively to their communities.

Our initiative is being developed from the local community in Kyenjojo District. We aim to listen to learners, young people, families, schools and community stakeholders as we shape realistic programmes around local priorities.

Our commitment to supporters

We will communicate clearly about our current stage, planned activities, the use of any support received and the progress we can verify.

We will not claim results, partnerships or registration that have not yet been achieved.

Accountability matters

Current status
